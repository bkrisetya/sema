Sema

A private assistant that lets authorised members of the Setyamarta household work with their own connected services.

Sema can connect to Google services after an account owner grants permission. It can help with tasks such as reading and organising mail, checking calendars, managing files, and working with contacts and task lists.

Privacy policy Terms of service

Connected services

Access depends on the scopes granted to each Google account. Sema does not request Google Maps access. Sema’s account has a wider service set than the other authorised household accounts.

The site itself does not provide a sign-in form, collect Google data, use advertising trackers, or sell information.
