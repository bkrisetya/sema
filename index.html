Sema Google

A private assistant that lets authorised members of the Setyamarta household work with their own Google services.

Sema acts only on an account after its owner grants Google OAuth access. It can help with tasks such as reading and organising mail, checking calendars, managing files, and working with contacts and task lists.

Privacy policy Terms of service

Google access

Access depends on the scopes granted to each Google account. The app does not request Google Maps access. Sema’s account has a wider service set than the other authorised household accounts.

The site itself does not provide a sign-in form, collect Google data, use advertising trackers, or sell information.
