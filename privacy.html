Privacy policy

Effective date: 15 September 2026

This policy explains how Sema Google handles information when an authorised Google account is connected to Sema, a private assistant operated for the Setyamarta household. This policy applies to the Sema Google application and its Google API connections. It does not apply to Google’s own services, which are governed by Google’s policies.

What Sema does

Sema receives requests from authorised household users and carries out requested assistant tasks. Depending on the account and the consent granted, this can include reading or organising Gmail, checking and managing Calendar events, finding or working with Drive files, using Docs, Sheets, or Slides, managing Contacts and People, and managing Google Tasks. Sema’s account may also have access to Chat, Classroom, Drive Activity, Drive Labels, Forms, Sites, Meet, Apps Script, Analytics, Search Console, AdSense, Ads, YouTube, Photos, and Photo Picker when those scopes have been granted. Google Maps is not requested by this application.

Information accessed

When an account owner grants access, Sema may process:

  • the Google account identifier and basic account information needed to select the authorised account;
  • Gmail messages, threads, labels, drafts, and settings permitted by the granted Gmail scopes;
  • Calendar events, calendars, attendees, and event details permitted by the granted Calendar scopes;
  • Drive files and metadata, and content from Docs, Sheets, or Slides, where the granted scopes and the user’s request permit it;
  • Contacts, People records, and Google Tasks permitted by the relevant scopes; and
  • information from other Google services only when the account owner has granted the corresponding scope and Sema needs it to answer a request.

Sema does not collect information from a Google service merely because that service is enabled in the Google Cloud project. Access is controlled by the account’s consented OAuth scopes and by the user’s request.

How information is used

Google data is used only to provide the requested assistant function, such as finding a message, preparing a draft, checking a calendar, managing a task, or returning information to the authorised user. Sema does not use Google user data for advertising, personalised advertising, credit or insurance decisions, or sale to data brokers. Google user data is not used to train a general-purpose artificial intelligence model.

Sharing and processing by providers

The Sema website does not receive Google account data. The Sema runtime processes data on a private, access-controlled host. When an AI service is needed to interpret a request or prepare a response, the minimum relevant context may be sent to the AI provider configured for Sema, solely to provide that response. Sema does not sell Google user data or share it for unrelated purposes.

Sema may disclose information if required by law, to protect the security of the service, or when the account owner explicitly directs it to a recipient as part of a requested action, such as sending a message or creating an invitation.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials are stored in an encrypted local keyring on the Sema host. The website does not store OAuth tokens or Google content. Sema does not intentionally maintain a separate cloud database of all Google content. Limited operational data, temporary caches, or excerpts may remain on the private host for as long as needed to complete a request, maintain reliability, or investigate a fault, after which they are removed under the host’s retention controls.

An account owner can request deletion of Sema-held data by contacting the address below. Revoking Sema’s access at Google Account permissions stops future API access; it does not delete copies that Google itself retains.

Security

Sema uses encrypted local credential storage, restricted host access, HTTPS for this website, and separate OAuth grants for the connected accounts. No website administrator or other household account is given access merely because another account has authorised Sema. No security measure can guarantee absolute security, so account owners should revoke access if they no longer want Sema to connect.

Children

Sema is a private household service and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children through this website.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Sema’s data handling changes. The effective date at the top will identify the current version. A material change will be communicated to the known household users where practicable.
