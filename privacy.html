Sema

Privacy policy

Effective date: 16 September 2026. This policy replaces the 15 September 2026 version.

This privacy policy applies to the Sema application and to this public information website at https://sema.krisetya.com/. Sema is a private household assistant. The developer is Beltsazar Krisetya. The organisation responsible for the application is the Setyamarta household. This policy does not apply to Google’s own products, which are governed by Google’s policies.

This policy is written for Google OAuth app verification as well as for household users. It discloses how Sema accesses, uses, stores, shares, retains, deletes, and protects Google user data. The privacy policy linked from the Sema homepage is the same privacy policy linked from the Google OAuth consent screen: https://sema.krisetya.com/privacy.html

Summary of data practices

Sema may collect, or process on behalf of authorised household account owners, Google user data after those account owners grant OAuth permission. Sema accesses only the data needed for a requested assistant feature. Sema uses that data to provide the requested feature. Sema stores OAuth credentials in an encrypted local keyring on a private host. Sema may send the minimum relevant context to a configured service provider solely to complete that requested feature. Sema does not sell Google user data. Sema does not transfer Google user data for advertising, credit, lending, or data-broker purposes. Sema does not use Google Workspace APIs to develop, improve, or train non-personalized artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.

What Sema is

Sema is a private assistant that lets an authorised Setyamarta household member work with that member’s own Google account. Sema is not a public consumer app, advertising platform, analytics product, or social network. This website describes the application. This website does not itself connect to a Google account or receive Google user data.

Google user data accessed by Sema

Our application will access the Google user data that an account owner has provided through Google and has authorised Sema to use. The categories of personal data Sema may collect, receive, or process when an authorised user interacts with Sema are set out below. Access is limited to the OAuth scopes granted for that account and to the data needed for the requested feature.

Account identity data: Sema may access the Google account identifier, email address, and basic profile information needed to select the correct authorised account and to show which account is in use.

Gmail data: Sema may access Gmail messages, threads, labels, drafts, attachments metadata, and mailbox settings when the granted Gmail scopes permit it. This can include message subject, sender, recipients, body, labels, and draft content.

Calendar data: Sema may access calendars, events, event titles, times, locations, descriptions, attendees, and availability when the granted Calendar scopes permit it.

Drive and document data: Sema may access Drive files, folders, file names, metadata, sharing metadata, and file content, including content from Docs, Sheets, or Slides, when the granted Drive or document scopes permit it.

Contacts and People data: Sema may access names, email addresses, phone numbers, postal addresses, and other contact fields stored in Contacts or People when the granted scopes permit it.

Tasks data: Sema may access task lists, task titles, notes, due dates, and completion state when the granted Tasks scopes permit it.

Additional household-account data: if the Sema household account has granted further scopes, Sema may also access Chat messages and spaces, Classroom course materials, Drive Activity records, Drive Labels, Forms responses, Sites content, Meet conference details, Apps Script projects, Analytics properties, Search Console data, AdSense and Ads account data, YouTube channel and video data, Photos libraries, and Photo Picker selections, and only when a requested feature needs that data. Google Maps data is not requested and is not accessed.

Sema does not collect Google user data merely because an API is enabled in the Google Cloud project. Sema does not scrape this website for Google account data. Sema does not receive Google user data until the account owner completes Google’s OAuth consent screen.

How Sema uses Google user data

We will use Google user data to provide the assistant services the authorised user requested. Examples include finding an email, preparing a draft reply, checking whether a calendar is free, creating or updating an event, locating a Drive file, editing a document, looking up a contact, or adding a task.

Google user data is used only to provide or improve user-facing features that are visible in Sema. Sema does not use Google user data to build marketing profiles, to serve advertisements, to determine credit-worthiness, for lending, to create a resale database, or to train non-personalized artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.

When an artificial intelligence service is used to interpret a request or prepare a response, Sema may include the minimum relevant excerpt of Google user data as context for that single response. That use is only to provide the requested user-facing feature. Sema does not use Google Workspace APIs to develop, improve, or train non-personalized AI or ML models.

How Sema shares, transfers, or discloses Google user data

We do not transfer or disclose Google user data to third parties for purposes other than the ones described in this policy. Sema does not sell Google user data. Sema does not provide Google user data to advertising platforms, data brokers, or information resellers. Sema does not transfer Google user data for targeted, personalized, retargeted, or interest-based advertising.

Sema may share the minimum relevant context with a configured processing provider solely to complete a requested assistant feature. Those providers act as processors for that task. They are not given Google user data to train their own general-purpose models on Sema’s behalf, and Sema does not authorise such training.

Sema may disclose Google user data if required by law, to protect the security of the service, to investigate abuse, or as part of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets after obtaining explicit prior consent from the account owner. Sema may also send Google user data to a recipient that the account owner explicitly directs as part of a requested action, such as sending an email or creating a calendar invitation.

Humans do not read Google user data obtained through Google APIs except where the account owner has given affirmative agreement to view specific messages, files, or other data; where it is necessary for security, such as investigating a bug or abuse; where it is necessary to comply with applicable law; or where data is aggregated for internal operations in accordance with applicable law.

Storage of Google user data

OAuth client credentials and refresh tokens are stored in an encrypted local keyring on the private Sema host. The website at https://sema.krisetya.com/ does not store OAuth tokens, Google account passwords, or Google user content. Sema does not maintain a separate public cloud database of all Google mailbox, calendar, or Drive content.

Limited operational data, temporary caches, logs, or excerpts may remain on the private host while a request is completed, while reliability is maintained, or while a fault is investigated. That stored material is Google user data or data derived from Google user data and is handled under this policy.

Retention and deletion

We store personal information for a period of time that is consistent with the purposes described in this policy. We will retain Google user data held by Sema for the length of time needed to fulfil those purposes unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law. When the retention period expires for a given type of data, we will delete or destroy it.

OAuth grants remain until the account owner revokes them or Sema deletes them. Temporary caches and excerpts are removed after the request is finished or after the host’s operational retention period, whichever is sooner.

You may request that Sema-held data be deleted by sending a request to beltsazar.krisetya.24 [at] ucl.ac.uk. Revoking Sema at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions stops future API access from that Google account. Revocation does not delete copies that Google itself retains in Gmail, Calendar, Drive, or other Google products.

How Sema protects Google user data

Security procedures are in place to protect the confidentiality of Google user data. We use encryption to protect OAuth credentials in the local keyring. The Sema host is access-controlled. This website is served over HTTPS. Each connected Google account has a separate OAuth grant. Another household account does not receive access merely because a different account has authorised Sema.

No security measure can guarantee absolute security. Account owners should revoke Sema’s access if they no longer want Sema to connect to their Google account.

Limited use of sensitive and restricted Google user data

Sema’s use of Google user data complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data obtained through Google APIs, and any data aggregated, anonymized, or derived from it, is used only to provide or improve user-facing features that are prominent in Sema. Transfers are limited to the cases described above. All other transfers, uses, or sales of user data are prohibited, including transferring or selling user data to advertising platforms, data brokers, or information resellers; using user data for serving ads; and using user data to determine credit-worthiness or for lending purposes.

Sema does not use Google Workspace APIs to develop, improve, or train non-personalized AI and/or ML models.

This website

The public website at https://sema.krisetya.com/ publishes information about Sema, this privacy policy, and the terms of service. It does not require a login. It does not collect Google user data. It may receive ordinary web server logs such as IP address, user agent, and requested URL from the hosting provider. Those logs are used only for hosting, security, and reliability of this information site.

Children

Sema is a private household service and is not directed at children. This website does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Sema’s data handling changes. The effective date at the top identifies the current version. A material change will be communicated to known household users where practicable. The current policy will remain published at https://sema.krisetya.com/privacy.html

Contact

Privacy questions, deletion requests, or requests to revoke Sema-held data can be sent to beltsazar.krisetya.24 [at] ucl.ac.uk. The Sema homepage is https://sema.krisetya.com/ and the terms of service are at https://sema.krisetya.com/terms.html
